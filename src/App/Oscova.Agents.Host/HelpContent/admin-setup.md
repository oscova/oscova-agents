# Operator Setup

Operator Setup appears only when Oscova starts with no users. Create the installation's one operator, or restore a backup before anyone signs in.

> **In plain terms:** this first-run screen decides who owns this installation.

## Create or restore

- Enter a valid email, a unique lowercase username of at least 3 characters, and a password of at least 6 characters.
- Click **Create operator** to provision the Operator account and continue to sign in.
- After signing in, create your Vault Password, then enter your preferred name during onboarding.

> **Note:** restore is blocked after any user exists.

Creating the operator waits while a restore is being prepared. Once the restore handoff is pending, account creation remains blocked until Oscova restarts or the previous environment is recovered.

## If setup fails

- Re-enter both password fields when they do not match.
