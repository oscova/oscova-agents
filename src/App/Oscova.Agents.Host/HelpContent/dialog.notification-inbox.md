# Notification inbox

Review saved notifications and open the work they refer to.

- **All** shows every entry.
- **Tasks** filters task notifications.
- **System**, when present, filters system notifications.
- Select a chat entry to open its conversation.
- Select a task entry to open the matching task and run.
- Low-priority notifications remain here even though they arrive without a toast.
- Other entries open their details.
- Plugin setup failures offer **Retry** after you correct the cause.

> **Note:** Reading an entry does not rerun its task.
