# Achievements

Use achievements to see which parts of Oscova you have discovered and the level your current progress has reached.

Open **Achievements** from your profile menu.

> **In plain terms:** XP is the point total from the badges currently shown on your achievement wall.

## Understand your progress

- The wall shows only achievements you have unlocked.
- Progress reflects your current setup and activity from the last 30 days.
- Chat achievements count your persisted conversations while the vault is unlocked. Incognito conversations are excluded.
- New unlocks reach the notification bell after one or two metrics refreshes.
- Turn off **Achievements and XP** in General settings to hide the feature and silence unlock notifications.

> **Note:** A badge can re-lock and reduce your XP when its underlying count falls below the requirement.
